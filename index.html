A genuine-looking health insurance request can still be fraudulent.

Remember to check:

  • The sender's actual email address.
  • The destination of links before clicking.
  • Whether the request was expected.
  • Whether sensitive information is necessary.
  • Whether HR or the HMO can verify the request independently.

Never provide your real NIN, insurance identifiers or medical details through an unexpected link.
